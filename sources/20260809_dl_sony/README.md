# Sony July 2026 proposal downloads README

The download was via the cimediacloud service (a MediaBox). There were seven files, two at a top level and five at a lower level. The two filenames of the top level files were equal to those of files in the lower level; and a check with md5sum showed that, in fact, the files at the lower level matched the ones at the upper level byte-for-byte. Therefore only a single level of hierarchy is used here to store the Sony contribution.
